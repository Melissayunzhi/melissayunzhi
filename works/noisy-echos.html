Noisy Echos

Jul 2024

Website created from scratch and using bodyPose model from ML5, with p5.js canvas

Visit: https://melissayunzhi.github.io/NoisyEchos/
(for initial set up: allow camera access, then refresh the page)

This interactive website brings to life Conway's cellular automata "The Game of Life".

Inspired by daily conversations and communication with people, I want to reflect on how our message can never be interpreted the same way to other people as to ourselves. Something is always lost, from the sender to receiver, the “noise” mentioned in Shannon Weaver’s Model of Communication.

Once finished with your message/drawing/text,  you will only be able to save the next three cellular automated generations of the your “message” on to your computer. Upload them to the Arena Hub and share your messages with each other.

This project is more than just an engaging visual experience.
I invite users to engage with this platform to enjoy the visual interplay and ponder the complexities of communication in our digital era.
