Luminescent Reverie

2023

January 2023

Delving into a captivating interplay of light and motion. The ever-changing patterns and harmonious organic forms evoke a sense of fluidity and motion, inviting the audience to explore the delicate balance between chaos and order.

Projected images created through Processing, watercoloured tracing paper hang from ceiling.
