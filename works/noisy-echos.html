Noisy Echos

Conway's Game of Life, set moving by your own messages: on the noise between what is said and what is heard.

Jul 2024

Conway's Game of Life, set in motion by the body. Visitors write a message, a drawing or a word with their own movement, tracked by ml5's bodyPose model, and then let the cells take over.

The work grows out of everyday conversation, and the way a message never arrives exactly as it was sent: the “noise” in Shannon and Weaver's model of communication. Only the next three generations can be saved and shared to an Are.na channel. After that, the message belongs to the noise.
