CCW Marketing Digital Ambassador

Showcasing the Inflatable Workshop at UAL

As the Digital Ambassador for CCW at UAL, I was tasked with creating a compelling reel to highlight the dynamics of the Inflatable workshop, a feature of the Fine Art: Computational Art course, held in May 2023.

My role involved filming and meticulously editing the footage to capture the essence of the workshop, all while maintaining consistent communication with the social media manager to align our vision.

Ultimately, the decision to pair the reel with a trending audio track was made, ensuring the final piece resonated with our audience and stayed current with social media trends.
