Flickring Home

Make a home using Flickr photo archive.

120 × 100 × 100 cm

A virtual home built from the Flickr archive. Photos tagged “home”, uploaded between 2004 and 2015, become the textures of a generic 3D room and change as you move through it. Visitors can type their own tags to furnish the space.

The work looks at how old personal photographs resurface or disappear, and which moments become the background of a personal history.
