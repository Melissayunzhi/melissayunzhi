Phreaking Collective

Co-founder, artist lead and co-curator of a collective of emerging computational artists.

Since Aug 2025

A group of emerging computational artists, co-founded in 2025. Phreaking organises self-initiated exhibitions, film nights, open projector evenings and talks with guest speakers.

As artist lead and co-curator, Melissa has taken exhibitions from first idea through install and deinstall: BitRot at Copeland Gallery, Does Cloud Compute (ever) Precipitate? at ANNEX by the Koppel Project, and Can We Start Again? at the old Archbishop Tenison's School site near The Oval, a show threading digital ecology, technological acceleration and the history of the site.
