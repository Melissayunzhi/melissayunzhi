Home is a dwelling path

Jun 2025

Interactive digital game, bunk-bed structure, ceramic bowl, and live one-to-one performance, 2025

The installation merges an explorative 3D environment with a physical install that mirrors its digital textures. Visitors navigate fragmented virtual “homes” built from personal stories on screen, constructed from their memories of home using personal images and found material on the Internet.
Together, the virtual realm and its tangible counterparts invite reflection on how we build and rebuild our sense of home across emotional and physical paths. After gameplay, participants are invited to the top bunk for an intimate “Pillow Talk” conversation, to tell their own home stories, and eventually develop the digital space further.

Visit web-space via link: https://newart.city/show/home-is-a-dwelling-path

The piece was shown at Digital Design Weekend 2025 at Victoria and Albert Museum, Peckham Digital 2025 at Copeland Gallery, Noiascape and APT exhibition.
